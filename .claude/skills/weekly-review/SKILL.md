# Weekly review

Keep the whole review short enough to read on a phone.

1. **Inbox.** Read `Inbox/Resources/inbox.md`. Move each item to the workstation it belongs to (add it to that workstation's resource file), then remove it from the Inbox. Ask only about items you cannot place.
2. **Next 7 days.** From Life Admin and the other workstations, list what is due or scheduled in the next 7 days, soonest first.
3. **Overdue.** List anything past its date and ask: done, reschedule or drop?
4. **Clean up.** If merged notes produced duplicate lines in a MEMORY.md or resource file, remove the duplicates.
5. **One new automation.** Look at what the user asked this week (Inbox items, memories, corrections). Propose exactly one new thing Claude could take over, and add it to `00_Resources/action-map.md` if they accept.
6. Close with a 3-line summary: done, coming up, the proposal.
